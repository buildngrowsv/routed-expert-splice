# Does native infill preserve a smaller coding donor’s lead when indentation changes behavior?

The delimiter study found a strong coding donor on syntax but all trained controls reached the ceiling. The variable-binding task kept every candidate executable but lost the standalone donor advantage. Test one bridge between these observations: completing executable indentation. A single token inserts 4, 8, 12 or 16 spaces before an addition inside a function with three nested guards or loops. Every candidate compiles. All four share the same preceding program. Four visible assertions encode the intended behavior, and at least twelve independently graded hidden cases distinguish the correct scope. Alter just the expected outputs of the visible assertions across a quartet to request each of the four scopes. Reject layouts whose visible or hidden cases fail to distinguish all four candidates. Require both original tokenizers to decode identical prompts and candidate completions.

Assign the 64 possible ordered triples of control-flow forms by fixed permutation to disjoint structural families for training (32), discovery (8), validation (12) and final test (12). Sample 1,536 training, 256 discovery, 384 validation and 384 final problems, evenly balanced by gold indentation. Resample whole control-flow families with their entire quartets. Run the frozen 0.5B coding donor and 1.5B base host on discovery only. If the donor leads by fewer than five percentage points, stop, retain the paired disagreement ledger, and keep the validation and final splits unscored. If it passes, continue to validation and train the native host’s block 28 with the same 96-update protocol and checkpoint selection as the earlier surgery. Require the donor to lead the trained host by five points with a positive lower family-bootstrap bound and the trained host to leave at least ten points of headroom before training the existing block-27 graft controls. If qualified, use the original matched arms, three training orders, and matched donor-off scoring of every new solve. Report raw counts, discordant directions, complete quartets and cluster uncertainty. An interpreter executing each legal candidate on the visible examples is a zero-training 100% control by design. The purpose is donor dependence under the controlled infill interface. A frozen lead alone cannot establish graft benefit.
