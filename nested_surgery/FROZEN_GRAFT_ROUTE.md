# Can uncertain 7B predictions benefit from the graft without calling it every time?

The intact specialist beat the graft on the decoy panel, but the two solved different groups. The graft solved 26 groups that the intact 7B missed. In an exploratory reanalysis of those 256 groups, blending one quarter graft logits into the intact specialist's logits on the bottom quartile of its top-two margin increased complete repairs from 150 to 162. The same exploratory operation made one fewer complete repair on the neutral layout. The earlier panel supplies the threshold and blend weight. These are discovery counts and do not qualify the method on their own. [Discovery evidence](learned_output_port_decoys_study/README.md)

Freeze both model checkpoints and the existing bridge. Take the absolute threshold from the 25th percentile of the archived decoy test's top-two raw candidate-logit margin and use that same threshold for every new prompt, with and without decoys. If the intact 7B margin is at or below the threshold, combine its logits with the graft's at weights 0.75 and 0.25. Otherwise keep the intact result. Train no weights and use no ground-truth information for routing. Both logits are already available when computing the graft. The additional cost relative to the intact model is the selected 14B forward pass. The comparison concerns paired candidate scoring and call fraction. Measure end-to-end latency separately before claiming a speed gain. [Score combination precedent](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/9ef2ed4b7fd2c810847ffa5fa85bce38-Paper.pdf) · [Frozen bridge](learned_output_port_study/README.md)

Use 768 fresh executable triplets at IDs 85000–85767, in both decoy and matched neutral layouts. Compare with the intact 7B, always using the graft, always blending, hard substitution on the same confidence mask, and the same number of randomly assigned blend calls averaged over 32 fixed draws. Score all frozen logits offline for matched controls. Execute the actual subset of host forwards in the first eight input batches to check that omitting other inputs reproduces graft candidate winners. Use a paired group bootstrap for the two primary differences.

Advance if on decoys the confidence blend beats the intact specialist by at least three points and the budget-matched random blend by at least two points, with both lower 95% bounds above zero. Require the neutral difference lower bound to be at least −2 points, the measured call fraction to be no more than one third on both layouts, and exact agreement of audited candidate winners. The result will concern one bounded synthetic repair task. The 7B already supplies the transferred features, so gains would support complementary processing by this fitted recipient, without implying independent statistical evidence or broader programming skill.
