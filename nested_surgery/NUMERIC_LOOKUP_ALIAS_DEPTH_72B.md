# Can a later layer use a copy of the original dictionary key?

At 17 of 80 blocks, exchanging one token's complete activation redirected both mirrored lookups in zero of 183 natively solved groups at the late copy. Swapping at the original numeral inside the same programs redirected 111. Earlier work found a large effect at a numeral declared after the table. A later hidden state could have acquired a causal role after the tested cut. This test compares the copy, its source, and the relocated declaration at fixed deeper cuts. [Single-cut result](numeric_lookup_alias_72b_study/README.md) · [Relocated declaration](numeric_lookup_fresh_72b_study/README.md)

Reuse the 256 paired groups in both archives. Score the late-copy read and its original source numeral on identical prompts. In a separately scored positive control, exchange the numeral when its declaration was moved after the dictionary. Prospectively choose 40 blocks (midpoint) and 60 blocks (three quarters), in addition to the archived 17-block result. At each position the donor prompt differs by one numeral, and every edited run exchanges an entire 8,192-value residual vector from the corresponding position. Replay the first batch of original-cut results and require identical target ranks and top candidate IDs. Compute newly intervened scores over the complete vocabulary. Frozen Qwen2.5-72B runs in BF16 with no quantization; the layout and candidate tokens are from the prior archives. [Implementation](identifier_lookup_alias_depth_72b.py)

Restrict the primary comparison to groups with all six native completions correct in both layouts. An edit must switch all four directional and mirrored completions. Score preservation on the remaining two constant-output completions. A deeper cut is informative if the relocated definition switches at least 80% and preserves at least 95% of constant-output groups. Call the alias usable at depth if at an informative cut it redirects at least 50%, preserves at least 95%, and gains 20 points over its original-cut rate with a paired lower bound above zero. Call the gap persistent if the upper bound on alias minus relocated definition is below −20 points at every informative deeper cut and at least one deeper cut qualifies. Use 8,000 paired group draws and central 97.5% intervals for the two deeper cuts. Report unresolved when the moved-definition controls close or the contrast misses its margin. The source location inside the copy prompts is a descriptive control scored at all cuts.

A selective response to a whole-token exchange tests whether the specified activation is sufficient to steer the answer, under this intervention and input family. It leaves other locations, projections and paths open. The paired design follows interchange-intervention work on causal abstraction and contextual binding.
