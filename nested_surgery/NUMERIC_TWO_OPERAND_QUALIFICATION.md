# Qualify a two-operand benchmark before repeating the transplant

The longer expression with multiplication and a constant left only eight clean groups in 1,024. Test one simpler task that keeps two independent operands and four distinct possible answers. Change the function body to return `left + right` or `left - right` directly. Require all four outcomes to be one-digit tokens absent from either operand's alternatives. All other controls and the exact seven-coordinate map stay the same.

First score only the unmodified 14B host on 128 fresh groups at indices 92000–92127. A group qualifies when each of its four programs ranks its executed answer strictly first among the four choices. If fewer than 20 groups qualify, record the native panel and stop. If at least 20 qualify, score the same six arms as the earlier test on 512 independent groups at indices 93000–93511, including left-only, right-only, both, equal-norm random and full-site exchange. The 7B programs are separate direct returns of their operands at indices 94000–94511 and 95000–95511. Use the original composition gate of at least 100 eligible groups, at least 60% success in each learned arm and the full-state control, and a lower paired confidence bound above 20 points over random for the both-operand edit. Preserve the complete screen regardless of the outcome.
