# Confirm the fixed confidence route on three times as many fresh repair groups

On 768 bracket-decoy groups, the fixed margin route combined the scores of the standalone 7B coder and its 14B graft on 22.92% of prompts. It solved 504 groups compared with 443 for standalone and 459.34 on average for matched random calls. It solved 646 neutral groups compared with 652 standalone. The neutral difference was −0.78 points (95% interval −2.60 to +1.04), leaving the registered two-point noninferiority requirement unresolved. The decoy result, matched-control comparison, call budget and audited sparse execution otherwise passed. This protocol increases the number of paired groups rather than choosing a different router.
