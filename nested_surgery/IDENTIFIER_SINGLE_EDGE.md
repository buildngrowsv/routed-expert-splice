# Can one block carry the edited declaration to the final token?

Direct reads after block 17 reproduced the unrestricted reversals. Earlier direct access by itself yielded zero. Select one block in the late interval on a separate calibration panel, then ask whether a single opening is sufficient and whether closing it removes the edit's effect.

Fit the established 25 directions on the original 512 single-declaration calibration pairs. On 64 distinct two-class groups at indices 104000–104063, propagate the original and edited source states with the direct edge to the final token blocked. At each block 18–48, compare the open and blocked final-token outputs for the same incoming state. Subtract the open-minus-blocked difference for the clean state from the corresponding edited difference. Choose the block with the largest mean displacement relative to the norm of its clean blocked output. Use only the query that selects the edited class. This calibration uses hidden states, not the correctness of any predicted answer. Lock the chosen block before scoring 384 new groups at indices 105000–105383.

Compare four availability schedules for that same direct edge: open throughout, closed throughout, open only in the chosen block, and closed only in the chosen block. Each schedule crosses a clean and edited source. Every other causal connection remains available. Use the intersection where all four original answers survive under all schedules. Require at least 100 such groups and a working unrestricted edit with at least 60% reversal in the edited class and at most 15% in the other class. The closed control must give at most 15% reversal in the edited class. Call the opening sufficient if it yields at least 70% reversal and its lower paired 95% bound relative to unrestricted is at least minus 15 points. Call the block necessary under this intervention if closing it lowers reversal by at least 20 points at the lower paired bound. Report both tests and the other-class results separately. The calibration evaluates the edge on blocked trajectories and selects a candidate mechanism. The held-out interventions supply the behavioral test. Estimate eight to ten minutes from the previous timing study.
